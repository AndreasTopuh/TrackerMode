I coded an app that LOUDLY Rickrolls me (and roasts me) every time I start doomscrolling.

How it works:
Using Python + OpenCV, I track face position, head tilt, and eye landmarks (via dlib, with an OpenCV fallback) to detect when I’m looking down at my phone for too long.

When doomscrolling is detected, it escalates fast:

- On-screen warnings
- Brutal roast messages
- RICKROLL
